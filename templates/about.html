About

Support

For help, questions, or feedback, email Tdmakepeace@gmail.com.

The app is built to help society admins run competitions and manage their society. It is not a general purpose app and is not intended to be used for other purposes. For access to the app, email Toby Makepeace at Tdmakepeace@gmail.com. He will create you an initial admin account. you can load competitions for your society; course details are shared so every club does not need to re-enter the same course.

User guide

  • Players sign in with the email the organiser registered for you and the competition password (one shared password per event, not a personal password). Open your competition to enter Stableford scores hole by hole.
  • Society admins add courses (pars and stroke indexes), create competitions on those courses, set the competition password players use, manage entrants and results, and invite other society admins.
  • Super admins create societies, lock or unlock them, and manage who can access the super-admin console.
  • Sessions end automatically after about 10 minutes without any activity; sign in again if you see a timeout message.
